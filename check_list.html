📅 Monthly Organic Farm Checklist – 2026

Crops covered: Arecanut (2+ yrs) Coconut Banana

January

CropTasks
Banana Harvest ongoing batches, remove dried leaves, continue monthly foliar spray
Arecanut General observation, remove weeds, no heavy inputs
Coconut Nut harvest, copra drying, observe button shedding

February

CropTasks
Banana Panchagavya spray (3%), foliar spray, remove excess suckers
Arecanut Basin cleaning in preparation for pre-monsoon
Coconut Clean manure circle, repair basins

March

CropTasks
Banana Foliar spray, monitor moisture stress
Arecanut Final basin prep, arrange compost & outside compost
Coconut Mulch with husk/leaves to conserve moisture

April (Pre-monsoon – Major Month)

CropTasks
Arecanut Apply compost (8 kg), outside compost (2 kg), neem cake (0.25 kg), Super-18 (1 ball), bio-agents
Coconut Apply compost (25 kg), neem cake (1 kg), Super-18 (2 balls), bio-agents
Banana Monthly foliar spray, Panchagavya (3%)
This is the most important soil-feeding month of the year.

May

CropTasks
Banana Ensure irrigation, foliar spray, watch leaf scorch
Arecanut Mulch basins, no additional inputs
Coconut Maintain mulch, observe flowering & nut set

June

CropTasks
Banana Panchagavya spray, foliar spray
Arecanut Weed control, drainage check
Coconut Ensure no water stagnation

July

CropTasks
Banana Foliar spray, remove dried leaves
Arecanut General monitoring, no soil inputs
Coconut Nut harvest if due

August (Mid-monsoon)

CropTasks
Arecanut Apply vermicompost (1 kg), Super-18 (1 ball), Panchagavya spray
Coconut Apply vermicompost (2 kg), Borax (25 g), MgSO₄ (0.5 kg)
Banana Pre-flowering vermicompost + Bilva Rasayana (if stage matches)

September

CropTasks
Banana Foliar spray, Panchagavya
Arecanut Observation only
Coconut Monitor nut retention

October (Post-monsoon)

CropTasks
Arecanut Apply Super-18 (1 ball), Panchagavya spray
Coconut Apply compost (15 kg), Super-18 (2 balls), Panchagavya spray
Banana Fruit development care, vermicompost if required

November

CropTasks
Banana Final Panchagavya spray, harvest planning
Arecanut Light basin reshaping
Coconut Harvest & copra drying

December

CropTasks
Banana Harvest remaining bunches, field cleanup
Arecanut Observation only, no feeding
Coconut General monitoring
December–January is a low-input observation period.
