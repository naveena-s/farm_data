🌱 Crop-wise Expense Audit & ROI Guardrails

🎯 Purpose of this document

This page answers three critical questions:

  • Where is money actually being spent per plant?
  • What is the minimum and maximum justified spend?
  • Where can we improve to increase income without increasing risk?
Rule of thumb:
Never spend more than 40–50% of a crop’s income on that crop.

🌴 Arecanut

Item ₹ / plant / year Notes
Fertilizers & micros ₹120 – 150 N, K, Mg, Boron are essential
Labour (basin, application) ₹100 – 120 Mostly unavoidable
Plant protection ₹60 – 80 Mahali & insects
Total spend ₹280 – 350 Income ≈ ₹1,050 / plant
✔ Arecanut gives predictable returns.
✔ Extra spending usually gives proportional yield.
Spend cap: ₹400 / plant
Beyond this → no economic return.

🥥 Coconut (Copra-based)

Item ₹ / palm / year Notes
Fertilizer (K, Mg, B heavy) ₹300 – 350 Controls nut number & size
Labour & harvest ₹180 – 220 Climbing, collection
Copra drying & handling ₹120 – 150 Quality protection
Total spend ₹650 – 700 Income ≈ ₹2,600 / palm
✔ Coconut is your income floor. Cutting cost here hurts future years.
Spend cap: ₹800 / palm

🌶️ Black Pepper (Panniyur-1)

Item ₹ / vine / year Notes
Plant protection ₹200 – 250 Major cost driver
Fertilizers & micros ₹180 – 220 K & Mg critical
Labour (training, harvest) ₹180 – 200 Seasonal peaks
Total spend ₹600 – 670 Income ≈ ₹1,500 / vine
⚠ Biggest overspend risk:
Calendar-based fungicide sprays without symptoms.
Spend range: ₹500 (min) – ₹750 (max)

🌿 Cardamom (Njallani Green Gold)

Item ₹ / plant / year Notes
Fungicides & sprays ₹350 – 450 Main cost center
Labour (frequent harvest) ₹300 – 350 Hidden cost
Fertilizers & micros ₹250 – 300 Yield stabilizer
Total spend ₹900 – 1,050 Income ≈ ₹3,000+
⚠ Cardamom costs escalate silently. Discipline is critical.
Absolute cap: ₹1,200 / plant
Beyond this → risk > reward.

☕ Coffee (Chandragiri)

Item ₹ / plant / year Notes
Labour (pruning, harvest) ₹80 – 100 Main expense
Fertilizer ₹70 – 90 Low response
Total spend ₹160 – 190 Income ≈ ₹200 / plant
Coffee is a stabilizer crop, not a profit engine. Overfeeding gives no ROI.

🍌 Banana (single cycle)

Item ₹ / plant / cycle Notes
Fertilizers ₹120 – 150 Main yield driver
Labour ₹80 – 100 Planting + harvest
Total spend ₹220 – 250 Income ≈ ₹400 / plant
Keep banana lean — short cycle, thin margins.

🔑 Final Takeaways

  • You are not overspending overall
  • Overspend risk exists mainly in pepper & cardamom sprays
  • Coconut & arecanut deserve consistent nutrition
  • Target total farm cost: 30–35% of gross income
Cost discipline here can unlock ₹2–3 lakh/year without increasing risk.
