🌱 Crop-wise Expense Audit & ROI Guardrails

🎯 Purpose of this document

  • Understand per-plant spend using real inputs
  • Define minimum, optimal, and maximum spend
  • Prevent silent cost creep while protecting yield
Core audit rule:
Perennial crops ≤ 30–35% of income
Annual crops ≤ 35–40% of income

🌴 Arecanut (2+ years, organic)

Cost head ₹ / plant / year Reality check
Compost + vermicompost ₹140 – 170 14–18 kg total, mostly farm-produced
Neem cake + bio-agents ₹20 – 30 Soil health & pest suppression
Super-18 balls ₹45 Growth response observed
Sprays (Panchagavya etc.) ₹15 – 20 Limited, not calendar-driven
Total spend ₹220 – 265 Income (steady): ₹1,200–1,400
✔ Spend is well within ROI limits
✔ Young-plant front loading is justified
Spend cap: ₹350 / plant
Beyond this → diminishing returns.

🥥 Coconut (copra-based)

Cost head ₹ / palm / year Reality check
Compost + vermicompost ₹320 – 380 40–45 kg total
Neem cake + Super-18 ₹100 Soil + micronutrient balance
Borax + MgSO₄ (once) ₹40 – 60 Corrective only
Total spend ₹460 – 540 Income: ₹2,600–2,750
✔ Coconut is the risk stabilizer crop
✔ Underfeeding hurts future yield
Spend cap: ₹650 / palm

🍌 Banana (single cycle, optimized)

Cost head ₹ / plant / cycle Notes (updated plan)
Compost + vermicompost ₹105 – 130 ~10 kg compost + 3 kg vermi
Neem cake ₹10 Soil conditioning
Bilva Rasayana ₹15 – 20 Used selectively
Foliar sprays (FAA + Panchagavya) ₹25 – 35 Strictly capped (LAST SPRAY rule)
Total spend ₹135 – 160 Income ≈ ₹400
⚠ Banana margins are thin.
Overfeeding quickly destroys ROI.
Target: ≤ 35% of income
Absolute cap: ₹170 / plant

🔑 Final Audit Verdict

  • Overall system is cost-disciplined
  • Arecanut & coconut are well-aligned
  • Banana is safe only if optimized plan is followed
  • No routine chemical dependency
Maintaining these guardrails can protect ₹2–3 lakh/year at current scale.
