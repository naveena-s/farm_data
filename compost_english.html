Final Premium Compost & Vermicompost Preparation – Practical SOP

Core Strategy
Bulk compost builds soil carbon and structure.
Vermicompost boosts biology and nutrient efficiency.
Enriched vermicompost delivers precision nutrition.
Not all compost is refined — only what is required.

1. Overall Production Plan

  • Two compost batches per year
  • 40 ft long windrow beds
  • 32–35 t bulk compost annually
  • ~25% converted to vermicompost
  • 6–7 t enriched vermicompost for precision use

2. Bulk Compost Preparation (Foundation)

Bed Size

  • 40 ft (L) × 6 ft (W) × 4 ft (H)
  • Raw material: ~8–9 t
  • Finished compost: ~6 t
  • Duration: 40–45 days

2.2 Layer-by-Layer Construction

  • Layer 1 – Base Aeration
    Coconut fronds / coarse dry matter (6 inches)
  • Layer 2 – Green & Wet
    Banana pseudostem + green leaves (8 inches)
    Spray cow urine 1:10 dilution
  • Layer 3 – Manure + Minerals
    Cow + goat manure (6 inches)
    Add oil cakes (25–30 kg), rock phosphate (6–7 kg), wood ash (2–2.5 kg), compost culture
  • Layer 4 – Carbon Balance
    Cocopeat + chopped fronds (6 inches)
    Spray decomposer solution
Repeat layers 2–4 for 3 cycles. Final height ≈ 4 ft.
Seal top with fine cocopeat / old compost (4 inches).

4. Vermicomposting (Refinement Stage)

  • Use only fully mature compost
  • Input: ~25% of bulk compost
  • Bed depth: 1–1.5 ft
  • Moisture: 50–60%
  • Shade mandatory

Worm Details

  • Eisenia fetida / Eudrilus spp.
  • 1 kg worms per 100 kg compost
  • Duration: 25–30 days
  • Output: 0.75–0.8 t vermicompost per tonne input

9. Storage, Safety & Quality Checks

  • Store under shade only
  • No direct sun exposure
  • Maintain 20–25% moisture
  • Do not add worms to hot compost
  • No ammonia smell
